About

I lead engineering at ANRA Technologies, an FAA-connected UAS Service Supplier. We build real-time drone traffic management: the systems that let operators like Amazon, DoorDash, and the FAA share airspace safely. My teams cover DevOps, platform reliability, and product delivery.

Before ANRA I led engineering at Apple (Project Titan), LendingTree, where we scaled to 72 engineers, CrossCountry Mortgage, Formant, and etailPet, and worked at Visa, Salesforce, and Yahoo.
